# EchoVault Privacy Policy

**Effective: 12 September 2026**

## Overview
EchoVault is a local-first Android audio journal. Recordings, transcripts, summaries, settings, and the timeline are stored on the user's device by default. EchoVault does not provide advertising, analytics, or user accounts.

## Microphone and local data
EchoVault uses microphone access only to provide recording features initiated or configured by the user. Audio and locally generated Whisper transcripts remain on the device unless the user explicitly exports content or chooses an optional cloud AI action.

## Optional cloud AI
Cloud AI is disabled by default. If a user enables it, supplies their own provider API key, confirms a request, and chooses to send transcript text, that text is transmitted over HTTPS directly to the selected AI provider to generate the requested response. Audio files are not sent by this feature. The provider may process or retain submitted text under its own terms and privacy policy. Provider API keys are stored using Android Keystore-backed encryption.

## Purchases
EchoVault may use Google Play Billing for optional in-app entitlements. Google processes payment information under Google's terms. EchoVault does not receive or store payment-card details.

## Sharing and exports
When a user chooses to export or share a recording, transcript, or backup, Android sends the selected content only to the destination chosen by the user. EchoVault does not operate a server that receives these exports.

## Retention and deletion
Local data remains on the device until the user deletes it, clears the app's storage, or uninstalls the app. Data sent to an optional third-party AI provider is governed by that provider's retention and deletion practices.

## Children
EchoVault is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed to children.

## Security
EchoVault disables cleartext network traffic, keeps app backups disabled, limits exported Android components, and encrypts stored provider credentials. No security measure can eliminate every risk.

## Changes
This policy may be updated when EchoVault's features or legal obligations change. The effective date above identifies the current version.

## Contact
For privacy or support questions, contact the EchoVault developer through the app's Google Play listing.
